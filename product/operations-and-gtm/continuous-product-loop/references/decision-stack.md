# Melissa Perri Decision Stack & Roadmapping Reference

## 1. The Build Trap vs. The Decision Stack

The **Build Trap** occurs when organizations measure product success by outputs (features shipped) rather than outcomes (customer behavior change and business impact).

The **Dynamic Decision-Making Stack** replaces static roadmaps with aligned strategic intent:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. VISION (5–10 Years)                                       │
│    Qualitative North Star: Who we serve & ultimate value     │
└──────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┘
                               │
┌──────────────────────────────▼───────────────────────────────┐
│ 2. STRATEGIC INTENTS (1–2 Years)                             │
│    Core business challenges preventing vision achievement    │
└──────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┘
                               │
┌──────────────────────────────▼───────────────────────────────┐
│ 3. PRODUCT PORTFOLIO (Ongoing)                               │
│    Problem & Value Stream Alignment (Customer Jobs & Friction)│
└──────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┘
                               │
┌──────────────────────────────▼───────────────────────────────┐
│ 4. PRODUCT INITIATIVES (Now-Next-Later)                      │
│    Measurable problem-solving bets against top obstacles     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. The Execution Kata

Every product initiative follows the Improvement Kata:
1. **Identify the Vision:** Where are we heading?
2. **Pinpoint the Challenge (Strategic Intent):** What business blocker must be overcome?
3. **Set the Target Condition:** What measurable customer behavior change must occur next?
4. **Benchmark the Current State:** Where are we today (exact baseline metrics)?
5. **Identify Obstacles:** What specific customer pain or friction blocks the target condition?
6. **Formulate the Bet (Initiative):** What is the smallest coherent solution hypothesis to test?
7. **Run the Experiment & Measure Evidence:** Did the target condition move?

---

## 3. Now-Next-Later Roadmap Structure

Unlike timeline roadmaps that promise artificial dates, the **Now-Next-Later** roadmap structures work by certainty and sequence:

- **NOW (Active Focus):** High-confidence, active initiatives currently in execution. Strict limit of 1–2 concurrent items.
- **NEXT (Solution Discovery):** Validated problem areas where discovery is complete and technical design is ready.
- **LATER (Exploratory / Vision):** High-potential problem spaces requiring UX discovery or market spike research before commitment.

---

## 4. The Required Strategy Sentence

Every strategy memo must feature this exact sentence:
```text
We will help [customer] achieve [target behavior/outcome] by solving [obstacle/problem], because it moves [business result], and we will prove it through [evidence/metric/experiment].
```
