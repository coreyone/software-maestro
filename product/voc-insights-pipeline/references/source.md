## 2. Multi-Channel Signal Ingestion Matrix

| Channel | Tool / System | Type of Data Collected | Ingestion Cadence | Primary Signal Extracted |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sales** | Salesforce / Gong | Win/Loss notes, competitor feature gaps | Daily | Purchase blockers, missing enterprise capabilities |
| **Account Management** | Salesforce / Gainsight | Churn exit surveys, upsell blockers | Monthly | Retention risks, unmet scale requirements |

## 3. The Usefulness Assessment Matrix (Fidelity Model)

Evaluate each user job across the product catalog:

| Ranked User Need | Product A | Product B | Product C | Synthesis / Status |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| *1. Save for recurring goals* | Fully Met (✓) | Not Met (✗) | Partially Met (~) | High satisfaction in core; gaps in bundle. |
| *2. Identify underperforming assets* | Not Met (✗) | Not Met (✗) | Not Met (✗) | **Major whitespace & customer churn driver.** |
| *3. Adjust strategy on priority change* | Partially Met (~) | Fully Met (✓) | Not Met (✗) | Usability friction in desktop experience. |

* **Rating Scale**:
  - **Fully Met (✓)**: Fast, intuitive, zero workarounds required.
  - **Partially Met (~)**: Functional but high friction, confusing UX, or manual export required.
  - **Not Met (✗)**: No capability exists; customers resort to third-party tools or churn.
