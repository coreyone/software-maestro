# Jake Knapp Design Sprint: Understand & Map (Monday)

## 1. First Principles & Mindsets
- **Start at the End**: Formulate the 6m–5y Long-Term Goal before discussing UI.
- **Pre-Mortem Inversion**: Invert risks into 3 precise, falsifiable Sprint Questions.
- **The Decider Rule**: Consensus dilutes vision; the Decider locks the single target.
- **Linear Journey Map**: Actors on the left, Goal on the right, 5–15 steps in between.

## 2. Step-by-Step Monday Execution Playbook
1. **09:00 - 10:00 | Long-Term Goal**: Define where the product will be in 1–3 years.
2. **10:00 - 10:45 | 3 Sprint Questions**: Invert critical failure modes into `"Can we...?"` questions.
3. **10:45 - 12:30 | Customer Journey Map**: Draw linear steps from discovery to value payoff.
4. **13:30 - 15:30 | Ask the Experts & HMWs**: Capture How Might We notes silently.
5. **15:30 - 17:00 | Pick the Target**: Decider places supervote on 1 Actor and 1 Step.
