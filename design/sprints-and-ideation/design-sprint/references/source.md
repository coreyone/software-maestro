# Jake Knapp Design Sprint: Understand, Diverge, Decide

## 1. Monday: Understand & Map
- **Start at the End**: Set a 2-year Long-Term Goal.
- **Sprint Questions**: Invert fears into 3 falsifiable questions.
- **Customer Journey Map**: 5–15 steps from discovery to target goal.
  - *tldraw Integration*: Call `tldraw-offline` to draw actor nodes, step rectangles, and bound transition arrows (`helpers.createArrowBetweenShapes(fromId, toId)`).
- **Pick the Target**: Decider selects exactly 1 target customer and 1 target moment on the map.

## 2. Tuesday: Diverge, Sketch & Tool Acceleration
- **Lightning Demos**: 3-minute presentations of great solutions.
  - *Stitch MCP*: Rapidly generate visual inspiration and moodboards with `generate_screen_from_text`.
- **4-Step Sketch**:
  1. Notes (20 mins review).
  2. Ideas (20 mins rough doodles).
  3. Crazy 8s (8 variations in 8 minutes on paper or `tldraw` canvas grid).
  4. Solution Sketch (3-panel self-explanatory storyboard with real titles and microcopy).

## 3. Wednesday: Decide, Storyboard & Token Synthesis
- **Sticky Decision Funnel**:
  1. Art Museum (hang sketches silently).
  2. Heat Map (dot voting on interesting elements).
  3. Speed Critique (3 mins per sketch).
  4. Straw Poll (each team member picks 1 favorite).
  5. Decider Supervote (Decider makes final binding choice).
- **10–15 Panel Storyboard Blueprint**:
  - *tldraw Wireframe Mode*: Render the 10–15 frame grid on canvas with caption boxes and connecting arrows.
  - *Stitch High-Fidelity Mode*: Format each panel with `.stitch/DESIGN.md` design tokens for Thursday's facade build.
