# Jake Knapp Design Sprint: Decide & Storyboard (Wednesday)

## 1. First Principles & Mindsets
- **Sticky Decision Funnel**: Eliminate debate bias through silent appraisal, heat maps, and supervotes.
- **Rumble Protocol**: Never hybridize conflicting paradigms into a Frankenstein product; test head-to-head.
- **Storyboard Law**: If it's not on the storyboard, it cannot be built on Thursday.
- **10-15 Panel Hard Cap**: Fewer than 10 creates gaps; more than 15 cannot be prototyped in 7 hours.

## 2. Storyboard Grid Architecture
- **Panel 1 (Opening Scene)**: Contextual discovery (Google Search, App Store, Inbound Email).
- **Panels 2-14**: Interactive golden path with real microcopy.
- **Panel 15 (Target Finish)**: Value realization and hypothesis resolution.
