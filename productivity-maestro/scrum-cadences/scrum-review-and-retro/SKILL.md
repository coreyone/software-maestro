# Rule: Scrum Sprint Review & Retrospective (Inspection & Adaptation Phase)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Lineage & Origins**: Grounded in **Jeff Sutherland & Ken Schwaber** (*Scrum Guide*), **Norm Kerth** (*Project Retrospectives*), and **W. Edwards Deming** (Systems Quality).
> **The Cybernetic Purpose**: Neutralizes **"90% Done" Theater, Watermelon Status Reporting, and Defensive Scapegoating**.
>
> **The Two Inseparable Adaptation Cadences**:
> 1. **Sprint Review (Reality Contact)**: Strict ban on PowerPoint slides. Inspects live, working, integrated software (the Increment) against the **Definition of Done (DoD)** with stakeholders, adapting the Product Backlog.
> 2. **Sprint Retrospective (Egoless Kaizen)**: Enforces Norm Kerth's Prime Directive (*"Everyone did the best job they could..."*), runs 5-Whys root-cause triage on friction, and commits to **strictly 1 measurable process upgrade** for the next sprint.

---

## When to use

Use this skill at the end of a sprint cycle:
- **Mode: `review`**: Auditing completed features against the Definition of Done (DoD), running the live working software demo, declaring Sprint Goal achievement, and capturing stakeholder backlog adaptations.
- **Mode: `retro`**: Facilitating a blameless Sprint Retrospective, running 5-Whys root cause analysis on system friction, and committing to **strictly 1 Kaizen process improvement**.

## Completion gate

- [ ] Definition of Done audit completed with binary verdicts for all sprint items.
- [ ] Sprint Goal achievement verdict declared (Achieved / Not Achieved).
- [ ] Norm Kerth Prime Directive affirmed with blameless 5-Whys root-cause analysis.
- [ ] Strictly 1 concrete, measurable Kaizen process upgrade defined for the next sprint.
