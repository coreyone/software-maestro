# Scrum Sprint Retrospective: Egoless Kaizen

## 1. First Principles & Psychological Antidotes
- **Blameless Prime Directive**: Bad outcomes are 94% system failures, 6% human error (Deming).
- **Rule of One**: Teams fail when they create 10 retro action items. Commit to exactly 1 measurable change.
- **Continuous Evolution**: Small compound improvements make the team $2\times$ faster over 6 months.

## 2. Retrospective Sequence
1. Set the Stage (Prime Directive).
2. Gather Data (Timeline of events).
3. Generate Insights (5-Whys).
4. Decide What to Do (Pick 1 Kaizen action).
5. Close the Loop.
