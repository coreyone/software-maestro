# Scrum Planning & Refinement Playbook

## 1. The DEEP Backlog Framework
- **Detailed Appropriately**: Top 2 sprints high detail (Gherkin criteria); bottom items broad themes.
- **Estimated**: Relative Fibonacci story points ($1, 2, 3, 5, 8$).
- **Emergent**: Living backlog pruned continuously.
- **Prioritized**: Linear $1 \dots N$ ordering by Cost of Delay (CD3).

## 2. Sprint Planning Invariants
- **1 Singular Sprint Goal**: Binds the team in shared fate.
- **Empirical Velocity**: Average of last 3 sprints minus on-call and OOO deductions.
- **Locked Backlog**: Zero scope creep injected mid-sprint without swapping equal points.
