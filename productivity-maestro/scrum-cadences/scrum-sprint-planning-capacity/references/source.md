# Scrum Sprint Planning: Cybernetic Capacity Calibration

## 1. First Principles & Psychological Antidotes
- **Optimism Bias Antidote**: Humans overestimate what they can build in 2 weeks. Historical velocity is the only valid predictor.
- **Decoupled Roles**: Product Owner decides *Priority (What)*; Developers decide *Commitment (How much)*.
- **Sprint Goal Cohesion**: A sprint is not a grab-bag of random tickets; it is an integrated push toward one outcome.

## 2. Planning Protocol
1. **Part 1 (Why & What)**: Product Owner presents the strategic objective; team refines the Sprint Goal.
2. **Part 2 (How & Commitment)**: Developers pull items matching capacity, estimate tasks, and commit.
