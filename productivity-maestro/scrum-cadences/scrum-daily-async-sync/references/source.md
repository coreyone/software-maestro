# Scrum Daily Standup: 24h Drift Triage

## 1. First Principles & Psychological Antidotes
- **Vulnerability Over Pride**: Admitting a blocker within 24 hours saves 2 weeks of lost time.
- **Peer-to-Peer Focus**: Speak to your teammates, not to a manager.
- **Sprint Goal Alignment**: Every task mentioned must directly advance the shared goal.

## 2. Daily Standup Protocol
- Strictly $\le 15$ minutes.
- Structure: Yesterday -> Today -> Blockers.
- Move technical debates to the "16th Minute" sidebar.
