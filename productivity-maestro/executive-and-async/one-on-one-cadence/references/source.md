# Canonical References & Operational Standards: High-Output 1:1 Cadences

This document outlines the management theory, psychological safety frameworks, and coaching principles governing effective one-on-one meetings.

---

## 1. Theoretical Foundations

### 1.1 Andy Grove's High Output Management (Intel)
- **Origin:** Andrew S. Grove (*High Output Management*, 1983).
- **Managerial Leverage:** A manager's output is the output of the organizational units under their supervision or influence. The 1:1 is the highest-leverage operational tool available to calibrate performance, uncover hidden organizational bugs, and transmit shared cultural values.
- **The Direct Report's Meeting:** The subordinate should prepare the outline and set the agenda. The supervisor's role is to facilitate the flow of information by asking clarifying questions.

### 1.2 Radical Candor (Kim Scott)
- **Origin:** Kim Scott (*Radical Candor: Be a Kick-Ass Boss Without Losing Your Humanity*, 2017).
- **Care Personally + Challenge Directly:** Effective feedback avoids both *Ruinous Empathy* (unwillingness to challenge directly) and *Obnoxious Aggression* (challenging without caring personally). Feedback must be immediate, specific, and focused on observable actions.

### 1.3 Camille Fournier's The Manager's Path
- **Origin:** Camille Fournier (*The Manager's Path: A Guide for Tech Leaders Navigating Growth and Change*, 2017).
- **Engineering 1:1 Dynamics:** Distinguishes between career coaching 1:1s, technical unblocking, and emotional venting. Focuses on preventing burnout, managing technical identity crises, and building long-term promotion cases.

---

## 2. Integrated Writing & Coaching Standards

- **ASD-STE100 Rules:** All action items start with imperative verbs (`Unblock`, `Introduce`, `Review`, `Escalate`). Sentences $\le 20$ words.
- **Info-to-Ink:** Drop conversational chit-chat from permanent debrief logs. Preserve exact deliverables and dates.
- **Observable Feedback Architecture:** State the Context $\to$ Observable Action $\to$ Business/Team Impact.
